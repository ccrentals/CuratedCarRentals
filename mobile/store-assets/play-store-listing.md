## Full description

Explore Jamaica with a carefully selected vehicle and local support from Curated Car Rentals.

Browse the live fleet, compare daily rates, passenger space and luggage capacity, check availability for your travel dates, and review a clear quote before reserving. The app shows the deposit due now and the remaining balance due on pickup.

Use Curated Car Rentals to:

- Browse live vehicles and rental rates
- Check date availability and minimum rental periods
- Choose pickup and return locations
- Review optional insurance and a complete price breakdown
- Sign and submit a reservation securely
- Pay the reservation deposit through WiPay's hosted checkout
- Save private booking access on your device and refresh reservation status
- Explore airport pickup, chauffeur, wedding, corporate and long-term rental services
- Discover Jamaican destinations and practical driving guidance

Curated Car Rentals serves Kingston and helps travellers enjoy Jamaica with dependable vehicles, straightforward pricing and responsive local support.
