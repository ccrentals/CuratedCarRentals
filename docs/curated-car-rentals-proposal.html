1) Executive Summary

This proposal outlines the high-level features, functionality, and third-party service costs for the Curated Car Rentals website. The goal is to deliver a professional, conversion-focused rental experience with a secure booking and operations workflow (including deposit payments, confirmations, and an admin portal).

2) Scope: Features & Functionality (High Level)

Public Website (Customer-Facing)

  • Modern marketing pages: Home, Fleet, Services, Tourist Destinations, About, Contact.
  • Fleet browsing with clear pricing, deposit information, and calls-to-action.
  • Mobile-first responsive layout with fast performance and SEO-friendly structure.

Booking Flow

  • Booking form collects key details (vehicle, name, email, phone, dates, pickup location).
  • Pricing breakdown shows deposit due now and balance due on pickup (with optional online balance payment).
  • Booking summary page with payment options (deposit, balance, or pay-in-full) and booking reference.
  • Payment result pages (success / failed) and customer support link.

Payments (Deposit + Balance)

  • Payment gateway integration for deposit and additional payments (balance / pay-in-full).
  • Webhook handling to confirm payments and keep booking status in sync.
  • Configurable fee handling (merchant-absorb vs other structures) depending on gateway agreement.

WiPay Online Payments (Implementation Notes)

  • Payments redirect customers to a hosted checkout page URL returned by the WiPay API.
  • After payment completion, customers return to a configured response_url with status + transaction details.
  • Typical return parameters include: status, transaction_id, order_id, total, message, and a verification hash.
  • Sandbox testing supports Visa/Mastercard test cards for end-to-end QA before go-live.

Emails + Invoices

  • Transactional emails for booking confirmations and reminders.
  • Invoice PDF generation and delivery (with graceful fallback if a PDF provider is unavailable or over quota).

Uploads (Vehicle Images)

  • Upload and manage vehicle images via a dedicated upload service/CDN.
  • Optimized delivery for faster Fleet browsing and improved user experience.

Admin Portal (Operations)

  • Secure admin authentication (sessions + CSRF protection; account lockout support).
  • Admin dashboard for day-to-day operations.
  • Manage vehicles, bookings, users, payments, reports, calendar/blockouts.
  • Operational tooling: readiness/health checks, and cron-triggered reminders (pickup / balance).
Note: The current implementation includes public routes (e.g., /, /fleet, /book) and admin routes (e.g., /admin, /admin/bookings, /admin/vehicles) as well as API endpoints for vehicles, bookings, health checks, and reminders.

3) Technology Overview (High-Level)

  • Frontend: Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript + Tailwind.
  • Backend (server routes): Next.js Route Handlers for public/admin APIs.
  • Database: Neon (Postgres).
  • Hosting/Deploy: Netlify (supports Next.js deployments + serverless/edge capabilities).

6) Project Fees (Not Included in Service Fees)

  • Development fee (one-time): $150,000.00
  • This covers the build of the website and core functionality described above. Third-party service fees are separate and billed monthly.

7) Development Timeline & Support

  • Estimated delivery timeline: 3 months (development + stabilization/support).
  • Monthly development (first 3 months): $20,000.00
  • Monthly support thereafter: $10,000.00 (*subject to change based on expected tasks to perform)

8) Payment Terms

  • 50% of the development fee is due after the first draft of the site is delivered.
  • The remaining 50% is due prior to go-live (after final review/sign-off).

9) Responsibilities & Go-Live

  • The site owner is expected to test all functionality before go-live (end-to-end).
  • Third-party accounts (Netlify, Neon, WiPay, Resend, PDFMonkey, Uploadcare) must be created/owned by the site owner.
  • WiPay live activation is subject to merchant onboarding/approval and provision of live credentials.

10) Assumptions & Notes

  • All vendor prices are subject to change; validate during implementation/go-live.
  • Payment processing is handled by the payment gateway; the site should not store raw card data.
  • Final ongoing costs depend heavily on traffic, booking volume, emails, PDF invoices, and image bandwidth.
